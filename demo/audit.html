How it works

  1. Label every photo onboard, with the model's chance for each class (about 60 bytes).
  2. Check a random sample: send some full photos, picked at random with known chances. Every photo keeps a minimum chance, so confident mistakes can be caught too.
  3. Correct on the ground: the labels' average, plus the average mistake seen in the checked photos. The error bar comes from the checks, not from the model's opinion of itself.
